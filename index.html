About - Dr Nick Cartwright

Dr Nick Cartwright is a coastal and hydrological scientist with over 20 years experience conducting research and teaching in the fields of coastal processes, catchment and groundwater hydrology. Nick has published numerous peer reviewed papers on coastal groundwater dynamics, ocean waves, coastal morphodynamics, regional hydrogeology and hydrology (see Publications page).

This site links to some interactive, web-based resources developed by Nick to support teaching and research activities in the fields of hydrology and coastal science.

  • Interactive Data Viewer/Analysis Tools
